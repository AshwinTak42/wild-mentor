Birding Experience

Rajasthan wetlands, grasslands and the forests of Thailand.

Birds, habitat and the stories behind them

In Rajasthan, winter brings migratory birds, including flamingos and pelicans, to wetlands such as the Jawai Dam. At Bijoliya, a grassland safari looks at vultures and the wider landscape they depend on.

Further afield, the Thailand Birding Expedition is a 5 night / 6 day trip through Thailand's national parks, from Bangkok to Bangkok, with flights, hotels and all meals included. It costs ₹1,00,000 per person.
