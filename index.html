The Mentor

Meet Ashwin Tak

Wildlife knowledge, field experience and a more meaningful safari

The Wild Mentor is Ashwin Tak's wildlife practice — built around personalised safaris, naturalist guidance, wildlife photography and a deeper understanding of the landscapes guests enter.

Wildlife Biologist · Naturalist · Safari & Wilderness Mentor

A trained naturalist with South African expert-guide training, Ashwin brings together animal behaviour, habitat interpretation, conservation work and storytelling to help guests understand more than just a sighting.

Naturalist & safari mentor

Personalised safaris, field interpretation, guest briefing and on-ground mentorship.

Wildlife biology

Reading animal behaviour, tracks, habitat and the ecological context behind each sighting.

Conservation fieldwork

Camera trapping, vulture work, wolf tracking, grassland conservation and field response.

Photography

Wildlife photography and visual storytelling that documents the landscape without losing its meaning.
